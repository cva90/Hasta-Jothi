COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I bring to a meditation session?

Wear comfortable clothing and bring a water bottle if you wish. A calm and open mindset is enough to begin.

Can beginners join the meditation programs?

Yes. The programs can be approached gradually, making them suitable for people who are new to meditation as well as regular practitioners.

How long is a meditation session?

Session duration can vary depending on the selected program and schedule.

Do I need previous meditation experience?

No previous experience is required. Beginners can start with simple breathing and mindfulness practices.
